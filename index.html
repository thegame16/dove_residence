Good to know

How do I book?

Use the booking planner below, or call or message us on WhatsApp, or send an email with your dates and the number of guests. We reply as soon as we can and confirm your booking.

What is furnished?

The bedrooms, kitchen and living room are all furnished. The house also has air conditioning, ceiling fans, a washing machine and a dining area. The kitchen comes with a fridge, gas cooker and microwave.

Is there parking and security?

Yes. There is a spacious car park inside the walled compound, and the property is covered by CCTV.

Is Wi-Fi and TV included?

Yes. Wi-Fi is free, and the house has DStv.

What about check-in and check-out?

We agree arrival and departure times with you when you book, so they fit your travel plans.
